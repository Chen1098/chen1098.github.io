Privacy Policy

Cheng Assistant · Last updated: September 29, 2026

Cheng Assistant ("the app") is a private, single-user automation tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It exists solely to help its owner — Chen Xu — manage his own email, calendar and files. It is not offered as a service to the public, and it has exactly one user: its owner.

1. What the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app accesses data belonging to the owner's own Google Account:

  • Gmail — read, send and organize the owner's own messages (e.g. to summarize them or send a message the owner has asked for);
  • Google Calendar — read and manage the owner's own calendars and events;
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — read and edit files owned by the owner;
  • Contacts — read the owner's own contact list.

Access is granted by the owner through Google's OAuth 2.0 consent screen and can be revoked by the owner at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

2. How the data is used

Accessed data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests on his own machine — for example preparing a daily summary of his own inbox, adding an event to his own calendar, or editing one of his own documents. The app performs no analytics, profiling, advertising, or any other secondary use.

3. Sharing and disclosure

Data is never sold, rented, transferred or disclosed to any third party. The app has no servers of its own and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Google user data is not used to train machine-learning models, is not used for credit or lending decisions, and is not read by humans.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored locally on the owner's own computer, protected by the operating system's file permissions. Nothing is uploaded to a remote service controlled by the app. The owner can delete this local data at any time, and revoking access in his Google Account immediately stops all further access.

5. Google API Limited Use

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone other than its owner.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Questions can be sent to chenxu1098@gmail.com.
